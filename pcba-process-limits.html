What the process can do, and what it only appears to do

Seven notes for the engineer still choosing footprints, panelisation and inspection. The subject is boundaries: what each inspection station actually proves, which limits are real, and the four different clocks hiding inside one lead time.

7 notes27 min end to endLast reviewed 17 Nov 2026

In 60 seconds

  1. 01SPI checks paste, AOI checks what is visible, X-ray checks what is hidden. None of them checks whether the board works.
  2. 02The placer is rarely the limit at 01005. Stencil, paste, pad geometry and inspection are.
  3. 03A DFM review is the last point where a change costs a file edit rather than a stencil, a scrap batch or a hand-rework line.
  4. 04A BOM that reads correctly can still be unbuyable — eight checks decide which one you have.
  5. 05Build time, material time, engineering time and transit time are four different waits. One number for four waits is how delivery promises break.
