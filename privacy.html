Privacy Policy

What Tenmay collects when you request a PCBA quote or browse this site, why, how long it is kept, and how to have it removed.

Last updated 17 November 2026|Version 1.0

1. Who is responsible for your data

This site, tenmaypcba.com, is operated by Shenzhen Tenmay Technology Co., Ltd. (“Tenmay”, “we”), a PCBA contract manufacturer registered in Shenzhen, Guangdong, China. We are the data controller for the information described below.

For any question, request or complaint about this policy, write to sales@tenmaypcba.com. We answer within five working days and aim to complete any request within 30 days.

2. What we collect

When you request a quote

  • Your email address, and a company name if you type one — the form works with an email alone.
  • The service type, quantity band and any project notes you write, which you should treat as visible to our engineering team.
  • The time the request reached us, so a reply can be traced back to it. The form does not send which page you were reading.

Files are not uploaded through this site. The form has no attachment field; when you reply to our answer you can send Gerber, BOM and centroid files by email or through the upload link we provide. Anything you send that way is covered by this policy.

When you browse

  • Aggregated analytics: pages viewed, approximate location (city level, derived from IP), device and browser type, referring site, and which buttons were clicked.
  • Technical logs kept by our hosting provider: IP address, timestamp and the file requested. These exist to keep the site online and to diagnose faults.

Analytics is configured so that the text you type cannot reach it: no email address, company name or project notes are sent to Google Analytics, on submit or at any other moment.

When you contact us directly

If you use the WhatsApp number, telephone number or email address on this site, we keep that conversation the way any supplier keeps correspondence — to answer you, quote, and follow up on an order.

3. Why we are allowed to use it

  • To answer a quote request and prepare a price — necessary steps before entering a contract with you.
  • To keep the site working, secure and honestly measured — our legitimate interest in running a business, balanced against your rights.
  • Analytics cookies and similar storage — only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To meet accounting, tax and export-control obligations once an order exists — legal obligation.

4. Cookies and similar storage

We use cookies for analytics and local storage for one thing: remembering your cookie choice. There is no advertising cookie, no remarketing tag and no social media pixel on this site. The full list, with names and lifetimes, is on the cookie page.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. It reaches the following recipients, and no others:

  • Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC — Google Analytics 4, for site measurement only.
  • Our hosting and email providers, which store the site and the mailbox that receives your request.
  • Component distributors and, if you ask us to, logistics providers — and only the details needed to price or ship an order you have asked for.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it, or where it is necessary to establish or defend a legal claim.

6. Where your data goes

We operate from Shenzhen, China, so a quote request is processed there. Some service providers — Google Analytics in particular — process data outside China and outside your own country. Where a transfer requires safeguards, we rely on the provider’s standard contractual clauses and on keeping the transferred data limited to what the service actually needs.

7. How long we keep it

  • Quote requests that do not become an order: 24 months after our last exchange, then deleted.
  • Analytics data: up to 14 months, as configured in our Google Analytics property.
  • Orders, invoices and shipping records: as long as accounting and tax law requires, which is longer than the periods above.
  • Server logs: a short rolling window set by the hosting provider, used only for security and fault diagnosis.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights over your personal data: to be told what we hold, to receive a copy, to have errors corrected, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to certain uses, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent for analytics at any time without it affecting anything else.

Use the contact address above; there is no form to fill in and no fee. We may ask you to confirm you are the person the data belongs to, but we will not ask for more than that. If you are not satisfied with our answer you can complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Security

The site is served over TLS. A quote request is held in access-controlled storage behind our intake endpoint, reachable only by staff who answer them. Design files you send are held on access-controlled storage and are not published, indexed or reused for another customer. We sign an NDA on request, before you send anything.

No system is perfect. If a breach ever affects your data, we will tell you and the relevant regulator as the law requires.

10. Children

This is a business-to-business site. It is not intended for anyone under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has sent us information, tell us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

The date and version at the top of this page change whenever the wording does. If a change materially affects how we use data we already hold, we will say so on this page rather than quietly republishing it. Continuing to use the site after a change means the current version applies.

Questions about this page

Write to sales@tenmaypcba.com and we will answer within five working days. Telephone and WhatsApp details are on the contact page.
