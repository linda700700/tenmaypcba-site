Why these four

Boards where the enclosure is the product and the electronics are the hidden half. Small batches, frequent revisions, no automotive paperwork.

Where the order is small, the reorder is predictable, and a three-day turnaround beats a cheaper quote from a larger plant.

Aftermarket parts do not require IATF 16949 — that is what makes this sector reachable. A 12 V accessory sold through retail is a consumer product with a car-shaped enclosure.

Same floor, same inspection, every family

Send the pack

A written DFM answer comes back inside one working day. Nothing is cut before you approve it.

What holds true across all twelve

Fifty pieces is a normal order

High-mix low-volume is the business, not a favour. Batch bands run from 50 to 5,000 with the same setup discipline on both ends.

Three days is the standard build

Counting from kitted parts, not from the quote. Long-lead components are called out before you commit, because they set the date.

No in-house ICT or test fixtures

Bed-of-nails and functional fixtures are made by a partner and wired back in. Boards that need full test coverage in one building are not a fit.
