About Tengmei

Eighteen years in Shenzhen,
building boards for other people’s products.

Shenzhen Tengmei Technology Co., Ltd. was established in 2008 and is registered in Shenzhen, Guangdong. We are a PCBA contract manufacturer: surface-mount and through-hole assembly, post-solder, conformal coating, programming and inspection, run across eight production lines of Yamaha YSM20 and YSM10 equipment.

We buy the bare board from qualified partner fabs rather than fabricate it, and we say so. That keeps the whole quotation with one team instead of three suppliers — which is where most of our price advantage comes from.

Quality is held at the line, not at the invoice: in-line SPI, AOI and X-ray on every shift, with first-article and per-lot records kept on file. High-mix work is the format we are built around, so a fifty-piece run is normal business here, not a favour.

Quality system

What is checked, where, and what is written down

Four stations, in one building, in the order a board meets them. Each one leaves a record that ships with the lot — that record is the product as much as the assembly is.

Ask for a sample record
01
Goods in

Incoming material

Bare boards are checked for warp, surface finish and panel markings against the order. Components are counted against the BOM, checked for date code and original packing, and moisture-sensitive parts are dried before they go near a line.

02
Before reflow

Print and first article

Solder paste height, volume and offset are measured on the 3D SPI after the first print, and the press parameters are written on the run sheet. The first assembled board is checked against the BOM with an LCR meter before the rest of the batch is released.

03
After reflow

AOI and X-ray

Every board passes AOI. BGA, QFN and through-hole fill are looked at on X-ray by an operator, not only by the machine, and the image is filed with the lot so a question a year later still has an answer.

04
Before packing

Final check and paperwork

Visual inspection to the IPC-A-610 class agreed on the order, firmware flashed and checksummed where that was quoted, boards packed to the drawing. A device history record and certificate of conformance go out with the lot.

Three typical builds

What the work actually looks like

No customer names and no yield percentages are published here — we do not have permission to publish them, and an invented number would be worse than none. What follows is the process behind three product families we build regularly.

BUILD 01Industrial control

Variable frequency drive — three boards, one order

Control board, IGBT power board and keypad board for a 380 V class drive.

The constraint

Three separate Gerbers, three stackups, one shipping date. Power boards need heavier copper than the control board and cannot share a paste profile with it.

What we do in the file review

All three files are reviewed together, so panelisation, tooling and the coating fixture are agreed once. Power and control boards run on separate profiles; keypad boards are coated selectively rather than dipped.

What that buys the buyer

A single PO covers the set, and the three boards leave on one schedule. Fifty sets upward is the volume this layout is built for.

THT power stage / selective conformal coating / 50 sets upward
BUILD 02Automotive aftermarket

Jump starter with air compressor

High-current power path, MCU control, compressor relay and a display board.

The constraint

Heavy copper on the power path, thick terminals that must be hand or selectively soldered, and a plastic housing that has to come back assembled rather than as loose boards.

What we do in the file review

Power and control sections are panelled for the same reflow pass; terminals are selectively soldered with a nitrogen nozzle so the SMT side is not re-heated. Box build and accessory kitting happen in the same building.

What that buys the buyer

Boards arrive as a tested sub-assembly, not a kit of parts, so the customer does not run a second assembly step.

Heavy copper / selective solder / box build and kitting
BUILD 03Consumer electronics

Treadmill motor control and console

Motor-drive power stage plus console and keypad, joined by a ribbon or harness.

The constraint

Firmware and layout change between revisions while the enclosure does not, so a revision can arrive when the previous one is still on the line.

What we do in the file review

Revision is bound to the machine program and to the programming station checksum, so a mixed-revision lot cannot be shipped silently. Small batches are scheduled as normal work rather than slotted around volume runs.

What that buys the buyer

A fifty-piece revision change is a normal order. Nothing waits for a bigger job to fill the line.

Double-sided SMT / device programming / 50-piece revisions
