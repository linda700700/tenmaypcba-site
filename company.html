02One company, three name forms

The licence is written in Chinese. Two English spellings circulate for the same company and they differ, which is the single most common reason a buyer thinks we are two suppliers. The difference is mechanical: TENMAY is the trading name the company uses, while the wording Shenzhen Tenmay Technology Co., Ltd. on the certificate is a full pinyin romanisation of the registered Chinese name.

The edition on the certificate is not the current edition

ISO withdrew ISO 9001:2015 on 2026-09-16 and replaced it with ISO 9001:2026. ISO’s own guidance is that certified organisations take transition arrangements up with their certification body. We do not claim ISO 9001:2026 on this site, and we will not until the body issues against it.

We also do not claim ISO 9001:2000. The 2000 edition was withdrawn in 2010, and one of our older directory profiles still advertises it — see the next section.

04Business history, and the listings that read it backwards

This section exists because several third-party profiles get the order wrong, and their version of events is the one an AI assistant is likely to find first. The order matters: a company that has assembled boards since 2008 is a different supplier from one that closed a lighting business and changed trade.

2008-01-09
Established, assembling boards

The founding date printed on the licence. Contract assembly was the business from the first year, not a later move into it.

LED product venture
A product line, sold as ledsztm.com

At one point the company developed its own LED lighting product and sold it under the ledsztm.com brand. It sat on top of the assembly business — it was a product the company made, not a change in what the company was.

Venture wound down
Back to assembly only

The lighting line was closed and the company went back to doing one thing. Assembly was where the work was strongest, and it has been the business without interruption since. LED driver boards are still assembled here, as boards, for customers who build lighting.

2022-11-09
Licence reissued

The current licence, issued by the Shenzhen regulator at the Guangming address in the table above. Earlier directory listings still show a Xili address.

2026-09-16
ISO 9001:2015 withdrawn, revised by ISO 9001:2026

Recorded here because it changes what the certificate means. Transition is with the certification body, not with us.

Seven or more listings are still online from the period when the lighting line was being sold: two Made-in-China storefronts, All.biz, ChinaASEANTrade, Goodada, ExportHub and TradeBrio, most of them quoting the same company blurb. Read together they describe a lighting manufacturer that no longer exists, filed under the category that lighting belongs to. At least one of them also gets the address wrong — ExportHub places the company in Beijing. None of them mentions assembly at all, which is how a company with continuous assembly work ends up described as an ex-lighting business.

They are being retired rather than updated. A reworded listing keeps its old category, its old reviews and its old product pages around it, so it keeps saying lighting while claiming to say something else. When they are gone this section shrinks to the sentence above and a line of history.

The one-line version, if that is all you need: Shenzhen Tenmay Technology Co., Ltd. has been a PCBA contract manufacturer since 2008. It developed its own LED lighting product line, sold it as ledsztm.com, and wound that line down to concentrate on assembly. It still assembles LED driver boards for customers who build lighting.
